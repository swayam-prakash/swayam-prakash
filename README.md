# Swayam Prakash, PhD

Immunologist and vaccine scientist | Assistant Researcher, Department of Ophthalmology, School of Medicine, University of California, Irvine

My research asks why some people control a virus without becoming ill, and how to build that response on purpose. I work on conserved B-cell, CD4 and CD8 T-cell targets for pan-coronavirus vaccines, tissue-resident memory T cells in herpes simplex infection, and human immunogenetics (KIR, HLA and transplantation).

**Research areas**
- Broad respiratory-virus vaccines (mRNA-LNP, adenoviral, AAV and peptide platforms)
- Tissue-resident memory T cells and prime-pull-keep immunotherapy
- Immunogenetics, histocompatibility and kidney transplantation
- Single-cell and bulk transcriptomics and immunoinformatics
- Artificial intelligence and data science: interpretable models of immune states and AI in vaccine design (MIDS, UC Berkeley; MSc in AI, Woolf, 2026)
- One Health and public health, including CKDu field studies and the Global Burden of Disease Study
